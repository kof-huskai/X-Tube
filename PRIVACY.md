# Privacy Policy — X Tube

**Last updated: 2026-11-09**

X Tube is a browser extension that runs locally in your browser.

- **Your Google AI Studio (Gemini) API key** is stored only in your browser (`chrome.storage`). It is never sent to us or to anyone except Google's API.
- **Video titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails and captions** you ask X Tube to analyze are sent directly from your browser to Google's Gemini API (`generativelanguage.googleapis.com`) to produce results. Google's own terms apply to that processing.
- **Keyword suggestions** use Google/YouTube public suggest endpoints.
- X Tube has **no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking**. We collect and store nothing.
- Permissions are used only for: reading YouTube / YouTube Studio pages you open (to show tags and fill title/tags/description), the side panel, and saving your settings.

You can remove all stored data at any time by using **Reset** in Settings or uninstalling the extension.
